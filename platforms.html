The platforms I work with

Easyshul

Extensive hands-on implementation experience

A cloud-based platform covering accounting, fundraising, yahrzeits, events, education, and pastoral care, with flat-rate pricing. A good fit for congregations that want one predictable monthly cost and don't have staff to spare for a long implementation.

ShulCloud

Worked at ShulCloud as main trainer and financial expert, and built its training protocols

The most widely used synagogue management platform, and the one I know most deeply. Strong for congregations that need a full membership, billing, and portal system and have the staff capacity to run it properly. Dedicated ShulCloud page.

ShulWise

Certified ShulWise consultant

One of the first AI-native synagogue platforms, built from the ground up for the AI era rather than layered on twenty-year-old foundations. I'm a certified ShulWise consultant, and I can help you decide whether it's the right fit for your congregation.
